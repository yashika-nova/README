## About Me

I am a **Project Associate at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee**, with a Master's degree in **Theoretical Nuclear Physics** and a strong foundation in **Computational Physics and Quantum Computing**.

My Master's thesis focused on *Investigating Multifragmentation through Transport Simulations and Fragment Recognition Algorithms in Nb–Nb Collisions*, exploring nuclear reaction dynamics through computational modelling and numerical simulations.

My research experience spans **nuclear structure models**, including the **Particle–Rotor Model (PRM)** and **Nilsson Model**, with applications to proton emission and single-particle energy spectra.

I am also actively working in **Quantum Computing and its applications to Nuclear Physics**, particularly quantum algorithms, variational methods, and **real-variance-based Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) approaches for Open Quantum Systems**, using **Qiskit**.

Previously, I completed a research internship at **IIT Roorkee**, focusing on nuclear models and quantum computing algorithms.
